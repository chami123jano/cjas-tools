Fast or exact? Videos are stored as occasional complete pictures ("key frames", usually every 1 to 5 seconds) with only the changes in between. The fast way copies the video as it is, so it takes a second and loses nothing, but it has to start at a key frame — the clip may begin up to a few seconds early. The exact way rebuilds the video, so it starts precisely where you said, but takes longer.
