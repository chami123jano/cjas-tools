Photo Collage

Two to nine photos in one picture. Your photos stay on this device.

Choose photos (several at once), or drag them here Add more any time · up to 9

Each photo is trimmed to fill its box, cutting a little off the sides or the top and bottom as needed and keeping the middle. Put a photo in a box shaped like it — a tall photo in a tall box — to lose the least. Use the arrows on each photo to change the order.

The collage is saved 2000 pixels on its longest side: sharp on any phone and good for printing up to about 6 × 6 inches.
