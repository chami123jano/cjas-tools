Nothing here will contact you. There are no notifications, no emails and no messages — it all stays on this device, which is also why it cannot chase you. Treat it as a list you check now and then, and put the real deadline in your phone's calendar for anything expensive to miss.

Why the warning periods differ. A passport renewal can take weeks and some countries refuse entry within six months of expiry, so the default is generous. Insurance you can usually sort out in a day. Set each one to however long you actually need.
