Text Encryption

Locked and unlocked on your device. The passphrase never leaves it.

There is no way back without the passphrase. Not for you, not for me, not for anyone. If you forget it the message is gone permanently — that is the whole point, but it does mean writing the passphrase down somewhere safe matters.

Uses AES-GCM with a key stretched from your passphrase using rounds of PBKDF2. The encrypted text also carries a check, so an edited message fails to unlock instead of quietly producing something wrong.
