Rotate and Flip Video

Turn a sideways phone video the right way up, or mirror it. Your video stays on this device.

Choose a video, or drag one here MP4, MOV, MKV, WEBM, AVI, 3GP

The preview turns straight away; saving rebuilds every frame the new way round, which takes a while for a long video. The sound is kept. The result is an MP4, which plays everywhere.

Mirror is for selfie videos where writing came out back to front.
