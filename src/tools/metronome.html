Metronome

A steady beat for practice. Press space to start and stop.

100beats a minute · Moderato

Tap: press Tap (or the T key) along with a song four or more times and the tempo is set to match.

The beat stays exact even if the computer is busy: each click is booked a moment ahead on the sound card's own clock rather than timed by the page.

Practising slowly works. Play a hard passage at a speed where it is clean, then raise the tempo by 5 at a time.
