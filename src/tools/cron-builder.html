Cron Expression Builder

Write it or read it, and check when it actually runs.

0minute
9hour
*day of month
*month
1-5day of week
—

Worked out in your computer's own time zone.

Five fields: minute, hour, day of month, month, day of week. Use * for every value, */5 for every fifth, 1-5 for a range and 1,3,5 for a list.

One trap worth knowing. If you set both day of month and day of week, cron runs when either matches, not both. So 0 0 1 * 1 runs on the 1st and every Monday, which is rarely what people mean.
