Bass and Treble

More bass, clearer voices, or a buzzing hum taken out. Your file stays on this device.

Choose a sound file, or drag one here MP3, WAV, M4A, OGG, FLAC · or a video, for its sound

Bass is the deep sounds under 250 Hz, middle is where voices sit (around 1 to 3 kHz), and treble is the sparkle above about 4 kHz.

Hum is the low buzz picked up from mains electricity — from a laptop charger, a fan or a cable near a microphone. It sits at exactly 50 Hz in Sri Lanka (the mains frequency), plus 100, 150 and 200 Hz, and those exact notes are cut very narrowly so the rest of the sound is hardly touched.

If boosting makes the loudest moments too loud, everything is turned down together just enough to avoid crackling.
