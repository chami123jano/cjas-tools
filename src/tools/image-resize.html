Resize Image

Make a picture bigger or smaller in pixels. Your picture stays on this device.

Choose a picture, drag one here, or paste one JPG, PNG, WebP, GIF or BMP

—After
—New file size

Making a picture smaller always looks good. It is shrunk in halves, step by step, which keeps fine lines and text sharper than shrinking in one go.

Making it bigger cannot add detail that was never there — it will look soft. For printing, it is usually better to print the original at a smaller size.

Unlocking the shape lets width and height change separately, which stretches or squashes the picture. To change the shape without stretching, use Crop Image instead.
