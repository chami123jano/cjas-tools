Why overlap. A fact that straddles a chunk boundary is lost to search, because neither chunk contains the whole of it. Repeating the last part of each chunk at the start of the next keeps those facts findable, at the cost of storing a little more. Around an eighth of the chunk size is a reasonable starting point.

Token counts here are a rough estimate from character counts. The Token Counter explains why an offline page cannot be exact about that.
