Join Audio

Several clips into one file, in the order you choose. Your files stay on this device.

Choose sound files (several at once), or drag them here MP3, WAV, M4A, OGG, FLAC · mixed kinds are fine

A crossfade overlaps the end of one clip with the start of the next, one getting quieter as the other gets louder — the way radio stations and DJs move between songs. The total is a little shorter than the clips added up.

Mono and stereo clips can be mixed; the result is stereo, with mono clips played in both ears.
