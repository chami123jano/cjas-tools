Remove Pages

Click the pages to take out, then save. Your file stays on this device.

Choose a PDF, or drag one here Any size

"Pick blank pages" looks at each page's picture and picks the ones with almost nothing printed on them — the empty backs of a double-sided scan, for example. Check the picks before saving: a page with only a faint line of text may look blank.

The original file is not changed. A new PDF is saved without the removed pages; everything else is kept exactly, nothing redrawn.
