A missed day does not reset everything. The current streak counts back from today and stops at the first gap, but the longest streak you have ever managed is kept separately — so one bad week does not erase the record of a good month.

Today is outlined rather than filled until you tick it, so an untouched day reads as "not yet" rather than "failed".
