Screen Recorder

Record your screen to show someone how to do something. The recording stays on this device.

0:00

Press start, then choose a whole screen, one window, or a browser tab.

What gets recorded is only what you choose in the box that appears: a whole screen, one window, or one browser tab. To share a tab's sound, tick "Share tab audio" in that box; for a whole screen on Windows, "Share system audio".

Hide anything private first — open messages, passwords, bank pages.

WebM or MP4? WebM is ready at once and plays in browsers, VLC and WhatsApp Web. MP4 plays everywhere, including phones and PowerPoint, but has to be rebuilt, which takes a while for long recordings. Recordings are not kept after you close the page.
