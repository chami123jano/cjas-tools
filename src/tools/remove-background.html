Remove Background

For plain backgrounds: a signature on white paper, a logo, a product on a plain sheet. Your picture stays on this device.

Choose a picture, drag one here, or paste one JPG, PNG, WebP, GIF or BMP

— found from the edges · click the picture to pick another

This works by colour, so it needs a background that is one plain colour and different from the subject. It cannot cut a person out of a busy room — that needs an AI service, which would mean uploading the photo.

"Only where it touches the edges" keeps white inside the subject — the white of an eye, a white logo on a red shirt — because those areas are not connected to the outside. Choose "everywhere" to also clear holes, such as the inside of the letter O in a signature.

For a signature: photograph or scan it on plain white paper in good light. The result is a see-through PNG you can put on a letter or a form. Grey shadows from the paper need a higher "how close" setting.
