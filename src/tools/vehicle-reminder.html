Vehicle Reminders

The four things that catch people out, per vehicle.

—

Why these four. In Sri Lanka the revenue licence cannot be renewed without valid insurance, and for most vehicles not without a current emission test either — so one lapsed certificate blocks the others. Having all four dates side by side is the point of this page.

Service is by distance as well as date, so both are shown. Put in the odometer reading and the interval, and it works out which comes first.

Nothing here contacts you. It stays on this device and will not chase you, so put the real deadline in your phone as well.
