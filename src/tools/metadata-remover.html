Photo Metadata Remover

Photos carry where and when they were taken. This takes that out.

Click to choose a photo, or drag one here

How this works. The photo is drawn onto a blank canvas and saved again from the pixels alone. Everything that was not a pixel — GPS coordinates, camera model, serial number, the date and time, editing history — is simply not carried across.

Two things to know. Saving a JPEG again loses a little quality, because JPEG is lossy; choose PNG if that matters more than file size. And the original file on your device is untouched — this gives you a new copy to share.

The photo never leaves this device.
