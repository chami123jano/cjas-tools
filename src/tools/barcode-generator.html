Barcode Generator

Retail barcodes, with the check digit calculated for you.

Only retail barcodes are here, on purpose. EAN and UPC share one small, well-defined digit table that can be checked against any real product on a shelf — so what this draws is verifiably right. Code 128 and Code 39 need much larger lookup tables, and a single wrong row produces a barcode that looks perfect and will not scan. That silent failure is worse than not offering them.

The blank margins matter. Scanners need clear space either side to find the edges of the code. Cropping them off is the most common reason a printed barcode fails to read.

A number you invent is not yours to use commercially. Real product codes are allocated by GS1, and the first digits identify the country and company. These are fine for testing, labelling your own shelves, or internal stock systems.
