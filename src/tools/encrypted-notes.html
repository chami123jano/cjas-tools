Forget the passphrase and the notes are gone. There is no reset and no recovery, by design. Nothing is uploaded, so nobody can look them up for you.

The notes are encrypted before being written to this browser's storage, so someone with access to the machine still cannot read them. They stay on this device only, and clearing browser data erases them.
