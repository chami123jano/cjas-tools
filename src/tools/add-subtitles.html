Add Subtitles to Video

Words on screen that show everywhere the video is played. English, Sinhala and Tamil. Your video stays on this device.

Choose a video, or drag one here MP4, MOV, MKV, WEBM, AVI, 3GP

The picture above shows exactly how the subtitles will look at the moment the video is paused.

Burned in means the words become part of the picture, so they show on WhatsApp, Facebook, Instagram and every phone, with nothing to switch on. They cannot be turned off afterwards, so keep the original video.

Times can be written as seconds (5 or 5.5), minutes and seconds (1:05), or hours too (1:02:05).

Sinhala and Tamil are drawn with Noto fonts included in this tool, so letters join correctly on every device.
