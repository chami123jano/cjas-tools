Add Page Numbers

Number the pages of a PDF. Your file stays on this device.

Choose a PDF, or drag one here Any size

Leaving out a cover: with "leave out the first 1 page" and "first number shown" at 1, the cover has no number and the page after it is numbered 1 — as in most reports and books.

"Page 1 of 10" counts only the numbered pages.

Pages that were turned sideways still get their number at the bottom as you see the page, reading the right way up.
