Convert Video

Change a video to the kind of file you need. Your video stays on this device.

Choose a video, or drag one here MP4, MOV, MKV, WEBM, AVI, 3GP, FLV, WMV, MPEG

Instant or rebuilt? A video file is a box holding a picture stream and a sound stream. When the streams inside are already a kind the new box accepts, they are simply moved across: it takes seconds and loses nothing. When they are not, the video has to be rebuilt, which takes longer.

Which to choose? MP4 for sending, phones, TVs and social media. WebM for putting on a website. MOV for Apple editing programs. MKV if you only play files in VLC and want to keep every sound track and subtitle.
