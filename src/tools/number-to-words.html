Number to Words

For cheques, receipts and legal documents, where the amount has to be written out.

Lakhs and crores. In Sri Lanka and India big numbers are grouped differently: one lakh is 1,00,000 (a hundred thousand) and one crore is 1,00,00,000 (ten million). So 2,50,00,000 is "two crore fifty lakh" here and "twenty-five million" elsewhere — the same amount either way. Commas can be typed in either style, or not at all.

On a cheque, cents are written out too, and the line ends with "only" so nothing can be added after it. Draw a line through any space left over.

Works up to 999 quadrillion, which is more than any cheque book allows.
