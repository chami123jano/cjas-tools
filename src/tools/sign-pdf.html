Sign PDF

Put your signature on a form or letter without printing it. Your file stays on this device.

Choose the PDF to sign, or drag it here Any size

Sign on plain white paper, take a photo, and make the paper see-through with Remove Background first, so only the ink shows on the PDF.

Click the page where the signature goes, then drag it into place; drag the round handle to make it bigger or smaller.

This is a picture of your signature, like signing a printed page and scanning it. It is not a "digital signature" with a certificate, which some government and bank systems require; if a form asks for that, this will not do.

Your signature is not kept. Nothing is saved or uploaded; close the page and it is gone.
