Crop Image

Drag the box to choose the part to keep. Your picture stays on this device.

Choose a picture, drag one here, or paste one JPG, PNG, WebP, GIF or BMP

Moving the box: drag inside it. Resizing: drag a corner. With a shape chosen, the corners keep that shape. For an exact size, type the numbers in pixels underneath.

Cropping only removes; the pixels you keep are not changed at all. To make the result a particular size afterwards, use Resize Image.
