Two ways to save: WAV plays everywhere and keeps every detail, but a minute is about 5 MB. WebM is about a twentieth of the size, plays in browsers, VLC and WhatsApp Web, but not in every older program.

Recordings are not kept after you close the page, so save the ones you want. Nothing is sent anywhere.

For clearer sound, keep the microphone about a hand's width from your mouth and record in a room with soft things in it — curtains, a bed, a sofa.
