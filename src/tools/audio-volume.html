Decibels (dB): +6 dB is about twice as loud on the meter, and about +10 dB is what sounds "twice as loud" to the ear. 0 dB on the loudest point is the most a sound file can hold.

Crackling happens when the loudest moments are pushed past that limit and get cut off. "As loud as it can go" raises everything just to below it. It cannot make a whisper louder than the shouts in the same recording; it raises both together.
