Speed and Reverse

Slow a lecture down to write it out, speed a voice note up, or play it backwards. Your file stays on this device.

Choose a sound file, or drag one here MP3, WAV, M4A, OGG, FLAC · or a video, for its sound

"Keep the voice natural" changes the speed without making voices squeaky or deep, by cutting the sound into tiny overlapping pieces and spacing them closer together or further apart. It is made for speech; music with strong beats can sound slightly fluttery at big changes.

"Like a tape" simply plays everything faster or slower, so the pitch moves with the speed. Twice as fast is an octave higher.
