Favicon Generator

The little icon in a browser tab, from a picture or from a letter.

Choose a picture, drag one here, or paste one Square works best. Anything else is centred and fitted.
My website

Which files you need. favicon.ico is the one every browser looks for; it holds the 16, 32 and 48 pixel sizes inside one file. The 180 pixel PNG is what an iPhone uses when someone adds your site to the home screen. The 192 and 512 sizes are for a site with a web app manifest, which lists them for Android. Put them all in your website's top folder.

At 16 pixels, detail disappears. A bold letter or a simple shape reads far better in a browser tab than a shrunken photo or a full logo.
