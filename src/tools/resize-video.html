Resize Video for Social Media

Upright for Status and Reels, square, or wide for YouTube. Your video stays on this device.

Choose a video, or drag one here MP4, MOV, MKV, WEBM, AVI, 3GP

Which shape? Upright 9:16 for WhatsApp Status, Instagram Reels, TikTok and YouTube Shorts. Tall 4:5 for Instagram and Facebook posts. Square for posts anywhere. Wide 16:9 for YouTube and TV.

Which way to fit? Cutting off the edges fills the screen but loses the sides (or top and bottom) — check the preview that nothing important goes. The blurred background keeps the whole picture and looks finished, the way most phone apps do it. Black bars keep it all, plainly.

The preview shows the moment the video is paused at; play or drag to check other moments.
