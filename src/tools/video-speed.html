Video Speed

Slow motion, faster, or a time-lapse. Your video stays on this device.

Choose a video, or drag one here MP4, MOV, MKV, WEBM, AVI, 3GP

Slow motion works best on videos filmed at a high frame rate (many phones have a "slo-mo" setting at 120 or 240 frames a second). From an ordinary 30 frames a second video, each picture is simply shown for longer, so fast movement can look a little jerky.

Sound: sped-up or slowed voices keep their own pitch rather than going squeaky or deep. Above 4× speech is too fast to follow, so removing the sound is usually better.
