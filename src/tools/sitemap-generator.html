Sitemap Generator

Paste your page addresses, get a sitemap.xml.

A full address or a path starting with a slash both work. Blank lines and anything after a # are ignored, so you can keep notes in the list.

Where it goes. Put the file at the top of your site, as example.com/sitemap.xml, then add a Sitemap: line to your robots.txt. Search engines look there first, so you usually do not need to submit it anywhere by hand.

Priority is mostly ignored. Google has said for years that it does not use the priority or change-frequency values. They do no harm, and some other engines still read them, but do not expect them to move anything. Listing a page at all is the part that matters.

The limits are 50,000 addresses and 50 MB per file. Beyond that you need several sitemaps and an index listing them.
