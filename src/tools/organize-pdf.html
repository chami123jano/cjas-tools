Organise PDF

Drag pages into a new order, turn them, delete them or add blank ones. Your file stays on this device.

Choose a PDF, or drag one here Any size

Under each page: ← and → move it one place, ↻ turns it, + adds a blank page after it, and × deletes it. On a computer you can also drag pages straight to a new place.

A blank page is the same size as the page before it — handy for making a double-sided print line up.

The numbers under each page are its place in the original, so you can see what has moved. Nothing is redrawn: text and quality stay exactly as they were.
