Merge PDF

Join several PDFs into one. Your files stay on this device.

Choose PDFs (several at once), or drag them here Pictures can go in too, as a page each

The order in the list is the order in the PDF. Use the arrows to move a file up or down. To use only some pages of a file, type them in its box: 1-3, 5 or 2- for page 2 to the end.

Nothing is redrawn, so text stays as sharp and searchable as it was in each original, and links inside a document keep working.

A protected PDF (one with a password, or marked "no changes") cannot be merged. Make an unprotected copy first: open it in your PDF reader, choose Print, pick "Microsoft Print to PDF" as the printer, and save.
