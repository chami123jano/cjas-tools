Which rates these are. The tax tables that came into force on 1 April 2025 under the Inland Revenue (Amendment) Act No. 2 of 2025: Rs. 1,800,000 a year tax free (Rs. 150,000 a month), then 6%, 18%, 24%, 30% and 36%. The Inland Revenue Department has not published new tables for 2026/2027, so these still apply.

EPF and ETF. You pay 8% of your gross into EPF. Your employer adds 12% to EPF and 3% to ETF on top of your salary — that money is not taken out of your pay and is not taxed as your income.

Please check before relying on it. This assumes one employer who you have named as your primary employment, a regular monthly salary, and no other relief or deductions. Bonuses, second jobs and terminal benefits use different IRD tables. Always confirm against your payslip or with your employer.
