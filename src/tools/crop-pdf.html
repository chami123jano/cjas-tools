Crop PDF

Trim the white margins, or cut pages down to the part you need. Your file stays on this device.

Choose a PDF, or drag one here Any size

Cropping hides the edges; it does not delete them. The trimmed parts are still inside the file, and some programs can show them again. To really remove something private, cover it instead with PDF to Images, then Pixelate or Blur, then Images to PDF.

Automatic trimming looks at each page and keeps everything printed on it, plus the space you choose. It suits slides, scanned receipts and PDFs with very wide margins for reading on a phone.
