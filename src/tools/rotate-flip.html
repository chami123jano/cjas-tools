Rotate and Flip

Turn a sideways photo the right way up, or straighten a crooked one. Your picture stays on this device.

Choose a picture, drag one here, or paste one JPG, PNG, WebP, GIF or BMP

Turning by quarter turns loses nothing — every pixel just moves to a new place. Straightening by a few degrees has to blend neighbouring pixels, which softens the picture very slightly, and leaves empty triangles at the corners: trimming cuts the picture down to the largest straight rectangle inside.

A tip for straightening: line up a horizon, a doorframe or the edge of a table with the edge of the picture.
