Audio Cutter

Keep just the part you want, or cut a part out. Your file stays on this device.

Choose a sound file, or drag one here MP3, WAV, M4A, OGG, FLAC · or a video, for its sound

Drag across the waveform to choose a part.

For a ringtone, choose about 30 seconds and add a 1 or 2 second fade at each end so it starts and stops gently.

The result is saved as WAV, which every phone and computer plays and which loses no quality. It is larger than MP3; MP3 saving arrives with the video tools.
