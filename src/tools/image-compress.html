How it gets smaller. JPG and WebP throw away detail the eye barely notices. Quality 75 to 85 is usually impossible to tell from the original; below 50 you start to see blocky patches around edges and in skies.

When a size limit cannot be met, making the picture fewer pixels across helps far more than lowering the quality further. A phone photo is often 4000 pixels wide; 1600 is plenty for a form or a screen.

See-through areas become white in a JPG, because JPG cannot store transparency. Use WebP to keep them.
