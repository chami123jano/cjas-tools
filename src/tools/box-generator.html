The SVG is drawn at true millimetre size, so a laser cutter or plotter will cut it correctly without scaling. Red lines are cuts, blue dashed lines are folds — most cutting software can separate them by colour.

Inside or outside measurements. With the box above ticked, the numbers are what has to fit inside, and the material thickness is added on so the contents actually go in. Untick it and the numbers become the outside of the finished box. Getting this backwards is the usual reason a box comes out slightly too small.

Score, do not cut, the fold lines. On card, run a blunt edge along them first; on a laser cutter, use a low-power pass. Cutting through them gives you five separate pieces.

Card up to about 1 mm folds cleanly with a straight score. Thicker material needs the fold line widened to roughly the thickness, or it will crack rather than bend.
