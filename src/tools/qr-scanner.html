QR Code Scanner

Decoded here on your device. No picture is uploaded anywhere.

The camera is off.
Use the buttons below.

Or click to choose a picture of a QR code, or drag one here

Nothing leaves your device. The camera image is read frame by frame in the page and thrown away — no picture is saved, and nothing is sent anywhere. The history above is kept only in memory and disappears when you close the page.

Be careful with what a QR code contains. A code is just text, and text can be a link to anywhere. A printed sticker on a parking meter or a restaurant table is trivially easy to replace with someone else's. This tool deliberately shows you the full address and makes you press a button, rather than opening anything for you — read it before you tap.

The camera needs permission, and on the website it only works over https. Reading a saved picture needs no permission at all.
