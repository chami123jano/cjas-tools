Business Name Ideas

A starting point, not an answer. Some of these will be terrible.

Nothing here is checked for availability, and nothing can be — asking whether a name is free means sending your ideas to a registrar or a trademark database, and nothing in these tools leaves your device. The domain is shown so you have something to paste into a search, not because it is known to be free.

Check the trademark register before you print anything. A name being available as a domain says nothing about whether somebody already owns it in your trade. In Sri Lanka that is the National Intellectual Property Office; a business name also has to be registered with the Registrar of Companies.

Say each one out loud. The names flagged above are ones you will end up spelling over the phone every time — a doubled letter where two words meet, or a sound with two plausible spellings.
