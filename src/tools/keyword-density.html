There is no target number. Search engines stopped rewarding a particular density many years ago, and stuffing a phrase in repeatedly now reads as spam to both readers and ranking systems. Anything above roughly 4% for a single word is flagged here, not because a threshold exists, but because it usually means the writing has gone stiff.

What this is actually useful for: checking that the subject you think the page is about appears at all, finding a phrase you have accidentally repeated nine times, and seeing which two and three word phrases a reader would come away with.
