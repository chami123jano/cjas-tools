Add Text to Image

Type your words, then drag them where you want them. Your picture stays on this device.

Choose a picture, drag one here, or paste one JPG, PNG, WebP, GIF or BMP

Size is a share of the picture's width, so the text looks the same on a small phone photo and a large camera one.

The fonts are the ones already on this computer. Sinhala and Tamil need a font that has those letters; Iskoola Pota and Nirmala UI come with Windows. If the letters show as boxes, the font is missing on this device.
