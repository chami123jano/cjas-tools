Image Splitter

Cut one picture into a grid of equal pieces. Your picture stays on this device.

Choose a picture, drag one here, or paste one JPG, PNG, WebP, GIF or BMP

For an Instagram grid, post the pieces starting from the last one (bottom right) and finishing with the first, because the newest post appears top left. The files are numbered in reading order, so post them from the highest number down.

For a panorama that people swipe through, post all the pieces together as one carousel in number order. Instagram crops carousel pictures to the shape of the first, so square pieces line up best.

The pieces come in a .zip file. Windows opens it with a double-click; on a phone, the Files app does.
