Image to ASCII Art

A picture drawn with letters and symbols. Your picture stays on this device.

Choose a picture, drag one here, or paste one Faces, animals and logos with clear shapes work best 

        

        

          
          
          
        

      


      

        
How it works: the picture is shrunk to one cell per
          character, and each cell's brightness picks a character — dense ones like
          @ and # for dark areas, a dot or a space for light ones. Characters are
          about twice as tall as they are wide, so the picture is squashed by half
          vertically first to keep its shape.
          


          When pasting it somewhere, it only lines up in a font where every
          character is the same width, such as Consolas or Courier. WhatsApp does
          this for text between three backticks: ```like this```.
