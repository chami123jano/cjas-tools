Watermark PDF

Stamp DRAFT, COPY, CONFIDENTIAL or your logo across the pages. Your file stays on this device.

Choose a PDF, or drag one here Any size

The watermark is drawn into the PDF, over the page, at the strength you choose. Light strengths (15 to 25%) leave the page easy to read.

It is not tamper-proof. Someone with PDF-editing software can remove a watermark. It is a clear label, not a lock. For a copy nobody can edit, turn the pages into pictures with PDF to Images and back with Images to PDF.
