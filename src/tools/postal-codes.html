Sri Lanka Postal Codes

Search by town name, or type a code to see which town it belongs to.

This covers Colombo District — 59 post offices. Other districts are being added. It was compiled from published directory listings, not from a live feed out of Sri Lanka Post.

Do not guess a code. Sri Lanka Post gives the same advice: if you are not certain which delivery office serves an address, check at slpost.gov.lk or ask your nearest post office before sending anything that matters.

For Colombo city, keep the leading zero — Colombo 3 is 00300, not 300.
