Hash Generator

A fingerprint of some text or a file. Nothing is uploaded.

Click to choose a file, or drag one here

Useful for checking a download arrived intact. Paste the hash the publisher gives and see whether it matches.

MD5 is not here on purpose. It is broken — two different files can be made to share an MD5 hash, so it proves nothing about authenticity. SHA-1 is included because old systems still use it, but it is also weak. Use SHA-256 unless something forces you not to.
