Video to GIF

A short moving picture from part of a video. Your video stays on this device.

Choose a video, or drag one here MP4, MOV, MKV, WEBM, AVI, 3GP

Keep it short. GIF is an old format that stores every picture almost whole, so a few seconds at a small size is best: 5 seconds at 480 pixels is usually 1 to 3 MB. Longer clips are better shared as video.

Colours: a GIF can hold only 256 colours, so the best 256 for this clip are worked out first, which keeps faces and skies looking natural.

GIFs have no sound.
