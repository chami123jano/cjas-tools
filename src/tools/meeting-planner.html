Meeting Planner

Green is a normal working hour where that person is.

The bottom row counts how many people are inside their own working hours at that time. Darker green is the best you can do.

Daylight saving is handled from your system's own time zone data, so a date in March or October gives the right answer rather than being an hour out. That is also why the date matters — the same people can be an hour further apart next month.

Each person can have their own working hours, because "nine to five" is not universal. Set someone to 6 and 14 if that is when they actually work.
