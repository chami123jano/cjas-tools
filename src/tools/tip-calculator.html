Check for a service charge first. Many restaurants and hotels in Sri Lanka add a service charge to the bill, usually 10%. If it is there, you have already paid something towards the staff, and anything more is up to you. Tick the box above and the tool shows what you have paid in service in total.

The tip is worked out on the food and drink only — not on top of the service charge, which would be charging a percentage of a percentage.

For splitting a bill where people had different things, use the Bill Splitter.
