Round Corners

Soft corners, or a circle for a profile picture. Your picture stays on this device.

Choose a picture, drag one here, or paste one JPG, PNG, WebP, GIF or BMP

See-through corners need a format that can store transparency, so the picture is saved as PNG — which is larger than JPG for photos. If the picture is only going on a page of one colour, filling the corners with that colour gives a much smaller JPG that looks the same.

WhatsApp and most social media already show profile pictures as circles and ignore see-through corners, so a plain square picture is fine there. The circle here is for documents, slides and websites.
