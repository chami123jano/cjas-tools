Join Videos

Several clips into one video, in the order you choose. Your videos stay on this device.

Choose video clips (several at once), or drag them here MP4, MOV, MKV, WEBM, AVI, 3GP · different sizes are fine

Clips of different shapes — an upright phone clip next to a wide one — are fitted inside the chosen size with black bars, never stretched or cut. Clips with no sound get silence, so the sound stays in step.

Every clip is rebuilt into one matching MP4, which takes a while for long clips. The browser has to hold all the clips at once, so keep the total under about 500 MB.
