Add a Border

A frame around a picture. Your picture stays on this device.

Choose a picture, drag one here, or paste one JPG, PNG, WebP, GIF or BMP

The border is added outside the picture, so none of the picture is covered and the result is bigger by the border on each side. The thickness is measured against the shorter side, so a border looks the same on a small picture and a large one.

Photo print leaves a deeper strip at the bottom, the classic place to write a date or a name.
