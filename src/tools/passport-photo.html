Passport Photo Maker

Frame your photo at the right size, then print several on one sheet. Your photo stays on this device.

Choose a photo, drag one here, or paste one Face the camera, in front of a plain light wall

Drag the photo to move it. Fit the top of the head to the upper dashed line and the chin to the lower one, with the eyes on the middle line.

Check the rules for your form first. Most passports, including Sri Lanka's, ask for a recent colour photo, face straight to the camera, neutral expression, eyes open, no glasses glare, and a plain light background. Sizes differ between countries and offices, so confirm the size on the application.

Printing: the sheet is made at 300 dots per inch, the standard for photo prints. Take it to any photo studio as a 4 × 6 print — usually far cheaper than passport photos made there — or print A4 at home and make sure the printer is set to "actual size", not "fit to page", or the photos come out the wrong size.

The background is not changed. If the wall behind you is not plain, take the photo again rather than editing it; offices can reject edited photos.
