Video to MP3

The sound from a video, as a music file. Also changes any audio file to MP3. Your file stays on this device.

Choose a video or sound file, or drag one here MP4, MOV, MKV, WEBM, AVI, 3GP · or M4A, OGG, OPUS, WAV, FLAC

The first time, the video engine (about 32 MB) is downloaded and kept by the browser; after that it starts straight away. In the desktop app it is already built in and needs no internet.

Which quality? 128 kbps is fine for talk and most listening; 192 kbps for music you care about. A higher number than the video's own sound cannot add quality that was not there.
