Pig Latin Translator

Move the first sounds to the end and add "ay". Type on either side.

The rules used. A word starting with consonants moves them all to the end: string becomes ingstray. "Qu" moves as one sound, so quick is ickquay. A Y at the start is a consonant (yellow → ellowyay), but later in a word it counts as a vowel, so rhythm becomes ythmrhay. Capitals and punctuation stay where they were.

Turning it back is a best guess without hyphens: eetay could have been tee or eet, and nothing in the word says which. With hyphens and the plain -ay ending, every word turns back exactly. (With -way, eird-way could be weird or eird.)
