NIC Number Decoder

Works on both the old 9-digit card and the new 12-digit one.

—Date of birth
—Age today
—Gender
—Born on a

How the number is built. The old card is nine digits and a letter: two digits of the birth year, three for the day of the year, three for a serial number, then a check digit and V or X. The new card is twelve digits: the full four-digit year, three for the day of the year, then five more.

The day-of-year carries the gender. Under 500 is male. For a female, 500 is added, so 532 means day 32 of the year.

One honest limitation. The numbering treats February as having 29 days in every year, including years that are not leap years. For a birthday from March onwards in a non-leap year, that can make the decoded date land one day out on some cards. This tool shows the alternative date when that applies. Where it matters, check against the date printed on the card itself.

This runs entirely on your device. The number is never sent anywhere.
