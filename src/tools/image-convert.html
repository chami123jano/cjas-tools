Which format? JPG for photos — small and opens everywhere. PNG for screenshots, logos and anything with sharp text or see-through parts; it never loses detail, but photos come out large. WebP does both jobs in a smaller file, and every current browser and phone opens it, though some older programs do not.

An animated GIF becomes a still picture of its first frame.

HEIC photos from an iPhone cannot be opened here, because Windows browsers cannot read them. On the iPhone, Settings › Camera › Formats › Most Compatible makes it save JPG instead.
