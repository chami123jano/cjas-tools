Alarm Clock

Rings on this device, as long as the page stays open.

--:--:--

This page has to stay open. An alarm here is a timer in the browser, not something your operating system knows about — so closing the tab or shutting the laptop stops it. For anything you genuinely cannot miss, use your phone.

If the page was closed over an alarm time, it will tell you the alarm was missed rather than ringing late and pretending it was on time.
