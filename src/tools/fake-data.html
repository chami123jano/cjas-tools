Test Data Generator

Rows that look real enough to test with, and are not.

These are invented, and some of them are deliberately invalid. Email addresses use example.com, which can never receive mail. Phone numbers use number ranges reserved for fiction. NIC numbers follow the right shape but are not real identities.

That is on purpose: test data that accidentally works is test data that can accidentally email a real person or charge a real card. If you need something that genuinely validates against a live system, that is a decision to make deliberately, not one to get by accident from a generator.

Why the data is correlated. A row where someone is 12 years old with a 20-year employment history is useless for testing, because real bugs hide in plausible data. Ages match dates of birth, cities match districts, and order totals match their items.
