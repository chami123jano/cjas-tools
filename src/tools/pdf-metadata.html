PDF Properties

See the hidden details inside a PDF, change them, or wipe them before sharing. Your file stays on this device.

Choose a PDF, or drag one here Any size

Why it matters: a PDF made from Word often carries the name of whoever the computer belongs to, the company name, and the exact time it was written — even when the document itself is anonymous. Anyone receiving it can see this under File › Properties.

"Wipe all details" removes the title, author, subject, keywords, the names of the programs used, the dates, and the separate block of hidden details (XMP) that some programs add as well.

The title is what a browser shows in its tab when the PDF is opened online, so it is worth setting on anything you publish.
