Video Sound

Remove the sound, replace it, or add music under it. Your files stay on this device.

Choose a video, or drag one here MP4, MOV, MKV, WEBM, AVI, 3GP

This is quick, even for long videos: the picture is copied exactly as it is and only the sound is changed.

The music fades out over the last two seconds so it does not stop suddenly. With "add music", 20 to 30% keeps voices easy to hear.

Music and copyright: songs you did not make may be blocked or muted when you upload the video to YouTube, Facebook or Instagram.
