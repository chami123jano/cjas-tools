Why 25 minutes. It is short enough that starting feels easy and long enough to get somewhere, which is the whole trick — the number matters far less than actually stopping when the break comes. Change it to whatever suits the work.

The timer compares against the real clock rather than counting down a variable, so it stays accurate when the tab is in the background, where browsers slow timers down.
