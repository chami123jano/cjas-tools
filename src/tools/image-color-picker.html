Photos are not as flat as they look. Even a plain blue sky is dozens of slightly different blues, so two clicks a few pixels apart can give different codes. The magnifier shows exactly which pixel is under the pointer.

For a logo or a website colour, paste a screenshot (Windows key + Shift + S, then Ctrl + V here) and click the flat area.
