Watermark Image

Your name or logo on a picture before you share it. Your picture stays on this device.

Choose the picture, drag one here, or paste one JPG, PNG, WebP, GIF or BMP

A corner mark is easy to crop off. If the picture really matters — a product photo, a property listing, artwork — repeat it across the whole picture at a low strength, around 20 to 30%. It stays readable to people but is hard to remove.

Keep the original. The watermark is part of the saved picture's pixels and cannot be taken off again.
