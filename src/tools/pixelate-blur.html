Pixelate or Blur

Drag over the parts to hide — a face, a number plate, an ID number. Your picture stays on this device.

Choose a picture, drag one here, or paste one JPG, PNG, WebP, GIF or BMP

For anything truly private, use the solid box. Light blur and small pixels can sometimes be partly reversed, or read by eye from a distance — especially numbers and text. Strong pixelation is safe for faces; a black box is the only sure choice for an NIC or bank number.

The saved picture is a new file with the hidden parts really replaced. Nothing underneath is kept, unlike covering something with a shape in a document.
