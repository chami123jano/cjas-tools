Base64 Encoder

Text or files, both directions.

Click to choose a file, or drag one here

Base64 is not encryption. Anyone can decode it in a second. It exists to carry binary data through places that only accept text, such as an email attachment or a data URL. Never put a password or a key in it and think it is hidden.

Accented letters, Sinhala and emoji are handled properly — the plain browser functions mangle anything outside basic Latin, so the text is converted through UTF-8 first.
