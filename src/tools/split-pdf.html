Split PDF

Take out the pages you need, or cut a PDF into several. Your file stays on this device.

Choose a PDF, or drag one here Any size

Typing pages: 1-3, 5, 8- means pages 1 to 3, page 5, and page 8 to the end. odd and even work too. You can also click the pages below to pick them.

"One PDF for each group" makes a separate file for every part between commas: 1-3, 4-10, 11- gives three PDFs.

Several PDFs come in one .zip file, so there is one download instead of many. Text, links and quality are kept exactly; nothing is redrawn.
