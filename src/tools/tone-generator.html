A steady tone at any pitch, or a sweep from low to high.

A4 — the note orchestras tune to

Start quiet. Very high and very low tones can be louder than they seem, and headphones put them right at your ears. 20% is plenty to begin with.

Checking your hearing: during the sweep, note the number when the sound disappears. Young ears hear up to about 17 to 20 kHz; it falls with age, which is normal. This is a rough check, not a medical test; small speakers also cannot play very high or very low tones.

Testing speakers: a pure tone that crackles or buzzes points to a damaged speaker. The low bass tones show whether a subwoofer is working.
