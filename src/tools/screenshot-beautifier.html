Screenshot Beautifier

Turn a plain screenshot into something you would put in a post or a slide. Your screenshot stays on this device.

Paste a screenshot (Windows key + Shift + S, then Ctrl + V), or choose a file PNG, JPG, WebP

Hide anything private first. Screenshots often show email addresses, phone numbers or account details in a corner. Blur them with Pixelate or Blur before sharing.

The saved picture is PNG, which keeps text in a screenshot crisp. It is made at the screenshot's own resolution, so nothing gets blurred by being shrunk and enlarged again.
