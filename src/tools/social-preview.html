Social Card Preview

The same link, as five different platforms will show it.

You can take these values straight from the Meta Tag Generator.

These are drawn from the numbers each platform publishes, not fetched from the platforms themselves. Nothing is uploaded and no request leaves your device, which also means this cannot tell you whether a platform has already cached an older version of your page. If you have changed your tags and a platform still shows the old card, use that platform's own debugging tool to make it fetch again.

One image for all of them. 1200 by 630 pixels works everywhere: it is the right shape for the large cards and gets cropped to a square reasonably for WhatsApp. Keep anything important away from the edges.
