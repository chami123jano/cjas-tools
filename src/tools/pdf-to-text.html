A scanned page has no words in it, only a picture of words, so nothing comes out for it. The count of "pages with no text" shows when this has happened. Reading words from pictures (OCR) is not part of this tool.

"Join lines into paragraphs" removes the line breaks the PDF put at the end of each printed line, so pasted text flows normally in an email or a document. Lines that end a sentence keep their break.

Columns, tables and footnotes come out in the order the PDF stores them, which is usually, but not always, the reading order.
