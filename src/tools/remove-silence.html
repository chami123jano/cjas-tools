—Pauses shortened

Pauses are shortened, not removed completely, so speech still sounds natural rather than rushed. The ends of each cut are faded over a few thousandths of a second, so there are no clicks.

If words get cut off, move "counts as silence" to a lower number such as -50 dB. If the background is noisy (a fan, traffic) and nothing is found, move it higher, such as -30 dB.
