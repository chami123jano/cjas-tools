Add Text to PDF

Type on any PDF, and tick boxes on printed forms. English, Sinhala and Tamil. Your file stays on this device.

Choose a PDF, or drag one here Any size

Click the page to add. Drag anything to move it; click text to change it; the red × removes it.

English text goes in as real text, so it can be searched and copied. Sinhala and Tamil are drawn with the fonts that come with Windows and placed as a sharp picture, so they show correctly in every PDF reader, even ones without those fonts.

For a fillable form — one where you can click in the boxes — Fill PDF Form fills the real boxes instead.
