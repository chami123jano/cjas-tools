Where the size goes: in most large PDFs it is the photos and scanned pages. This tool shrinks those to a sensible size and saves them more compactly. Text, fonts and links are left exactly as they were, so the words stay sharp and searchable.

A PDF of plain typed text is usually small already; there is little to gain, and the tool will say so rather than give you a bigger file.

"Turn every page into a picture" gives the biggest saving for scanned documents, but the result is pictures only: the text can no longer be selected, searched or copied. Keep the original.
