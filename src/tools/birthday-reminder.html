Birthday Reminder

Who is next, how old they will be, and how long you have.

—

If you do not know the year, pick any year and the age will simply be left out.

A 29 February birthday is shown on 1 March in years that have no 29th, which is the usual convention.

Nothing here leaves your device, so there are no notifications and no emails — this is a list you look at, not something that will chase you. Everything is kept in this browser only.
