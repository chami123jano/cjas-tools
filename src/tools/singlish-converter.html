Singlish to Sinhala

Type with English letters, get Sinhala Unicode you can paste anywhere.

If the Sinhala shows as boxes, your device has no Sinhala font. Windows has one built in — Settings, then Time & language, then Language, and add Sinhala.

Capital letters matter for a few sounds: t gives ට but th gives ත, d gives ඩ but dh gives ද, n gives න but N gives ණ, and l gives ල but L gives ළ. An n before k, g or h becomes ං, so lankaawa gives ලංකාව.
