Team Picker

Teams, or one name at a time without repeats.

—Press draw

The split is genuinely random, then evened out. The list is shuffled with Fisher-Yates and dealt round the teams one at a time, so eleven people across three teams gives four, four and three rather than five, three and three. Which team gets the extra person is itself random.

Drawing does not repeat anyone until the list is used up, so it works for a raffle or for deciding an order. The chips show who is still in.

Nothing is saved, so closing the page starts over.
